What this tool does

ClaimIQ reviews a claim form, repair estimate or FIR, and the customer's incident description against the motor insurance policy. It checks completeness, surfaces contradictions between documents, cites the policy clauses that apply, and recommends approve / reject / request information — escalating to a human investigator whenever the evidence does not support a confident conclusion.

The review workspace is under construction — sample claims and the full review flow arrive in upcoming phases.
